# Lab note — p4-pilot, what a real-provider POC 4 would spend (2026-11-10)

Crate: `lab/p4-pilot`. Track: lab (CON-23). **Every number here is exploratory and may
not be cited (CON-24).** No provider was called. The token counts are the mock's
(`mock-gated`, CON-26); the prices were read from the providers' pricing pages on
2026-11-10 and must be read again before any spend is approved.

## Question

GATE-14, POC 4 on at least two real providers, has been deferred twice, and with it the
choice of models and the spend estimate (ADR-29, ADR-42). Before asking the maintainer
for either:

1. Roughly what does the full frozen grid cost per provider, and what does the smallest
   useful pilot cost?
2. What stands between today's harness and a first real call?

## What was tried

**The estimator.** `lab/p4-pilot` reads a loop report and, for every bundle it names,
sums the tokens of the bundle's calls from `views/call.parquet`, in four kinds:
prompt tokens neither read from nor written to the cache, cache reads, cache writes,
and output. It groups bundles by provider slice and prices each group from a price
sheet (`lab/p4-pilot/prices/*.toml`).

**The input.** GATE-23's loop of record, `90f7fda2…`: 1 548 `sim` bundles of
`lab/hypotheses/p4-twin.toml`, which has POC 4's design. That is 387 bundles a
provider (384 grid cells and 3 controls), each of 20 replicates of one workload.

**Two sizes.**
- *The grid*: every bundle of a provider, as the frozen design asks.
- *A pilot*: per workload, the control and every cell exactly one knob away from it,
  at 5 replicates. That is 27 bundles a provider, a quarter of their replicates.
  It is not a loop and cannot give a verdict: `search` has no one-at-a-time design, so
  it would be 27 direct `acn harness run` calls a provider. It proves the wiring and
  shows each knob's direction on a real cache.

**The prices**, USD per million tokens, standard tier:

| provider | model | input | cache read | cache write | output |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| anthropic | `claude-haiku-5-5` (prompts up to 100 000 tokens) | 0.10 | 0.01 | 0.125 (5 min) | 0.50 |
| anthropic | `claude-sonnet-5-5` | 2.00 | 0.10 | 2.50 (5 min) | 10.00 |
| openai | `gpt-6-luna` | 0.10 | 0.01 | 0.125 (30 min) | 0.50 |
| openai | `gpt-6.1-sol` | 2.00 | 0.10 | 2.50 (30 min) | 10.00 |

Sources: <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing> and
<https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing>. The models are the cheapest and a
mid-priced current model of each provider; they are a proposal, not a pin.

## What was learned

### Tokens, by the mock's model of each provider

| provider (mock profile) | size | calls | uncached in | cache read | cache write | output |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| anthropic (`mock-explicit`) | grid | 258 123 | 343.4 M | 277.2 M | 228.2 M | 21.2 M |
| anthropic (`mock-explicit`) | pilot | 4 094 | 4.26 M | 4.43 M | 3.96 M | 0.31 M |
| openai (`mock-auto`) | grid | 248 123 | 383.0 M | 441.9 M | 0 | 19.7 M |
| openai (`mock-auto`) | pilot | 4 089 | 6.39 M | 6.25 M | 0 | 0.31 M |

The grid is about 850 million prompt tokens and 20 million output tokens a provider.
The pilot is about 1.5% of it.

### Spend, at those tokens and prices

| tier | provider | grid, USD | pilot, USD |
|---|---|---|---|
| cheapest | anthropic, `claude-haiku-5-5` | 76 | 1.12 |
| cheapest | openai, `gpt-6-luna` | 53 to 62 | 0.86 to 1.02 |
| mid | anthropic, `claude-sonnet-5-5` | 1 497 | 22 |
| mid | openai, `gpt-6.1-sol` | 1 007 to 1 199 | 17 to 20 |

The OpenAI range is "no cache write is billed" to "every uncached prompt token is
billed as a 30-minute write": see finding 3.

1. **On the cheapest current models the whole frozen grid is about $130 to $140 for
   two providers, by these counts.** That is small enough that the grid's size, which
   ADR-29 kept, is not a spend problem at this tier. On mid-priced models it is about
   $2 500 to $2 700.
2. **A pilot is about $2 on the cheapest models and about $40 on mid-priced ones.**
3. **The counts understate a real run, by an amount this note cannot give.** Allow a
   factor of two to three until a pilot has measured it:
   - *Output.* The mock's replies are 32 to 256 tokens and it does not think.
     `claude-haiku-5-5` thinks by default, and thinking is billed as output. Output is
     14% of the Haiku grid figure here; at ten times the output it would be most of it.
   - *Tokenizer.* The mock counts a token as four bytes. Anthropic's documentation says
     `claude-haiku-5-5`'s tokenizer counts the same text as about 30% more tokens than
     its predecessor's; neither is the mock's.
   - *Retries and failures* are not in the counts.
   - *The mock's caching is a model* (CON-26). A real cache has a minimum cacheable
     prefix, a time to live, and routing the mock does not have.

### What stands between the harness and a first real call

4. **The harness cannot call `claude-haiku-5-5` as it is.** It always sends
   `temperature`, and the P4 workloads set `0.0`. Anthropic's documentation for this
   model says a non-default sampling value is rejected with a 400. This is issue #27's
   first point, met by PR 90 (SPEC 040 v0.5, a workload may leave `temperature` out)
   and the code that follows it. Not tried against the API.
5. **OpenAI's current models list a price for cache writes.** The pricing page gives
   30-minute and 12-hour write prices for the `gpt-6` and `gpt-5.6` families, at 1.25
   and 2.5 times input for `gpt-6-luna`. It does not say when a write is charged.
   POC 4's premise for this provider, in PLAN §8b and in the `mock-auto` profile, is
   automatic prefix caching with no write premium, and `mock-auto` records no cache
   write. If writes are billed on the pinned model, the `openai` slice has a cost term
   the mock leaves out, and `cost_per_success` on it is wrong in a way that depends on
   the knobs. **This has to be settled from OpenAI's caching documentation before the
   `openai` model is pinned.** It was not settled here.
6. **A reply cut at its token limit is likely on a model that thinks**, with the P4
   limit of 256. That is issue #27's other two points, left open by PR 90 (SPEC 040
   §10, question 6). A pilot should record, per model, how many replies end
   `max_tokens` and how many tokens were asked for.
7. **`claude-haiku-5-5` has a higher price above 100 000 prompt tokens.** The largest
   P4 prompts are far below it; a workload that grew past it would change price
   mid-session.
8. **`acn loop promote` does not exist** (T06c), so a live campaign has no L3 runner.
   A pilot does not need one. The frozen grid does.
9. **No keys are set on this machine**, and no spend ceiling or model has been given.

## Graduate / park / drop

**Recommendation: run the pilot on the cheapest models, once three things are in
place.**

- PR 90 and its code, so a request can leave `temperature` out (finding 4).
- The maintainer's inputs: keys in the environment, a ceiling, and the two models. This
  note proposes `claude-haiku-5-5` and `gpt-6-luna`, and a ceiling of $25 for the
  pilot: about ten times its estimate, to cover finding 3 and a second attempt.
- A reading of OpenAI's caching documentation on cache writes (finding 5).

The pilot's lab note should give, per provider and model: tokens of each kind against
this note's counts; the share of replies cut at the limit; whether each knob moves the
cached share in the direction the mock predicts; and, for Anthropic, whether a rolling
breakpoint reads the previous request's prefix, which settles issue #32.

**For the campaign itself (GATE-14):** at the cheapest tier the estimate argues for
running the full frozen grid on two providers, at roughly $300 to $500 allowing for
finding 3, and deciding about a mid-priced tier only after it. That is the
maintainer's decision, with the pins (T06d).

The estimator stays in `lab/`. P4-10 asks for the spend estimate to be computed from
the L1 run of record on the pinned files; when that is done for the record it belongs
in `acn-cli`, over the same view.
