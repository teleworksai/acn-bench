# Lab note — p1a-atlas: POC 1a by hand, to find what SPEC 110 has to decide (2026-11-12)

Crate: `lab/p1a-atlas`. Candidate: `lab/hypotheses/p1a.toml`. Track: lab (CON-23).
**Every number here is exploratory and may not be cited (CON-24).** Mock backend
(`mock-auto`), guessed link numbers, four replicates a cell, one machine (macOS arm64).

## Question

TASKS.md gives POC 1a one line: a 3×3 grid of placement and profile, the 5% boundary, a
heatmap, and a REST workflow as control. Nothing else in the repository defines it. Before
SPEC 110 is written:

1. Can the run path produce the grid at all, in sim and in live, with a verdict and a twin?
2. What does it show on the mock, and what is missing before any of it could be a result?

## What was tried

**The grid.** Nine scenario files, `lab/p1a-atlas/scenarios/<placement>-<profile>.toml`.

| placement | one-way delay | round trip |
|---|---|---|
| `edge` | 2.5 ms | 5 ms |
| `regional` | 20 ms | 40 ms |
| `remote` | 80 ms | 160 ms |

| profile | what it adds to the delay |
|---|---|
| `wired` | nothing |
| `wifi` | 3 ms jitter, 0.5% independent loss, 50/100 Mbit/s |
| `cellular` | the stages of `scenarios/synthetic/cellular-handover.toml` without its handover: burst loss up, 0.2% loss down, 20/100 Mbit/s, 8/5 ms jitter |

**The arms.** Treatment: `workloads/agent.toml`, one task of four turns, local tools,
streamed answers of up to 64 tokens (about 1.3 s of mock model time a call). Control:
`workloads/rpc.toml`, four exchanges with no tools, no streaming and a one-token reply.
**The control is a stand-in.** The plain-RPC control of CON-18 does not exist (SPEC 050,
T13b); this one still goes through the harness and the mock.

**The driver.** `acn loop run` runs with no scenario (ADR-34), and it refuses a `workload`
control. So `lab/p1a-atlas` calls `acn harness run --scenario …` once per cell, arm and
mode, with `--vary placement=… --vary profile=…`, then `acn hyp verdict` over the 36
bundles, and `acn attrib heatmap`. `placement` and `profile` are not knobs: the harness
records them and ignores them, and the driver is what ties a pair to a scenario file.

Run, from the repository root, with `acn` built:
`lab/p1a-atlas/target/debug/lab-p1a-atlas --runs-dir <dir>/runs --modes sim,live`.
Sim takes 6 s; sim and live together took 305 s.

## What was learned

### It runs, end to end

Sim, live on the served mock through the proxy, a verdict with a `workload` control that
inherits both parameters, the twin rule and the heatmap all work on hand-made bundles
with one scenario per cell. Nothing in the frozen set had to change for that.

### The numbers (sim; share of turn time)

| cell | agent: network | agent: retry | stand-in control: network |
|---|---|---|---|
| edge, wired | 0.7% | 0 | 17.6% |
| edge, wifi | 0.7% | 27.4% | 17.8% |
| edge, cellular | 0.8% | 17.9% | 21.6% |
| regional, wired | 5.6% | 0 | 63.1% |
| regional, wifi | 4.2% | 27.1% | 63.1% |
| regional, cellular | 4.7% | 17.7% | 63.3% |
| remote, wired | 19.3% | 0 | 87.2% |
| remote, wifi | 14.3% | 26.1% | 87.2% |
| remote, cellular | 15.9% | 17.0% | 87.3% |

1. **On this workload the agent's network share crosses 5% at about 40 ms round trip.**
   The candidate's falsifier, `max_over_knobs(network_attributable_share) >= 0.05`, is
   true: sim verdict `fail`, decision cell `remote, wired`. Where the crossing falls is
   set by the mock's 20 ms a token and the 64-token answers, which are choices of this
   note. The share was not broken down by call.
2. **The agent's share is far below the stand-in control's on every cell**, because model
   time fills an agent turn. An `effect` against this control is negative everywhere
   (the heatmap draws that). "Agent minus plain RPC" may be the wrong way round for a
   sensitivity atlas; see the decisions below.
3. **Loss does not appear as network time. It appears as retries.** The emulator drops a
   message and nothing retransmits it (SPEC 020, EMU-5), so one lost chunk of a stream
   ends the call ("the network cut the stream") and the harness makes the call again.
   At 0.5% loss a message and a streamed answer of tens of chunks (the count was not read out), a quarter of the agent's turn time
   is `retry_share`, the same at every placement, and the network share goes *down* on
   the lossy profiles because the turn got longer. A TCP connection would have turned
   most of those losses into delay. As modelled, the profile axis measures the harness's
   retry policy.
4. **The stand-in control never retried**: four one-message exchanges a replicate are too
   few to meet a 0.5% loss in four replicates.
5. `ttft_p50_ms` is undefined on the three `wifi` cells in sim (at least one replicate has
   no value). Not looked into.

### The twin

Live against sim, absolute difference in network share, tolerance 0.02:

| | agent | stand-in control |
|---|---|---|
| edge | 0.004 to 0.005 | **0.056 to 0.070** |
| regional | 0.004 to 0.006 | 0.006 to 0.011 |
| remote | 0.002 to 0.003 | 0.016 to 0.017 |

6. **The agent arm twins well everywhere**, within 0.006.
7. **The control fails the twin on the three edge cells**, so the twinned verdict is
   `inconclusive` (`twin_failed`), although the decision cell is within tolerance. An
   exchange of the stand-in lasts about 30 ms at the edge. Live adds real time on
   loopback, in the proxy and in HTTP that sim does not have, and against 30 ms it
   shows. The live figures themselves were not read out; only the divergence was.
   **A short request/response control is where sim and live part first.**

### What SPEC 110 has to decide

- **What "placement" and "profile" mean**, with numbers that come from somewhere. These
  are guesses. The measured 5G trace is one profile that is not a guess.
- **What loss means on this link.** Either the scenarios of an atlas carry no
  message-level loss, or the emulator gets a reliable-transport stage (a SPEC 020
  change), or the atlas reports `network + retry` and says so. Finding 3 stands in the
  way of every lossy cell.
- **The control, and the direction of the comparison.** T13b's plain-RPC control needs
  its span in SPEC 010 first (Class C). Then: is the claim "an agent turn is less
  sensitive than a request/response call", or "the agent's share stays under a
  threshold"? The second needs no control to state, and CON-18 still wants one.
- **The quantity.** A share falls when the turn gets longer for any reason (finding 3).
  Added seconds a turn, or the share at a fixed model speed, may be the better atlas.
- **The model's speed is the hidden axis.** The crossing moves with tokens a second and
  answer length. An atlas on the mock is an atlas of the mock's profile.
- **The twin tolerance for short exchanges** (finding 7): absolute, per arm, or a floor
  on exchange length.
- **Scenarios in `acn loop run`** (ADR-34's deferred `env-change`), or the atlas stays
  hand-driven. A frozen file is `grid` only (HYP-9), so "the bisection driver for the 5%
  boundary" needs SPEC 080 §6 question 2 answered first, or the boundary is read off a
  finer grid.
- **Replicates and length.** Four replicates of four turns is a smoke test. A frozen
  file needs 20, and live time grows with it: this grid at 20 replicates is about
  25 minutes of live.

### Known biases

- Mock model, one profile (`mock-auto`), fixed 20 ms a token.
- Guessed link numbers; no outage; one-path scenarios; tools are local, so only the
  model hop is on the link.
- Four replicates; no interval is quoted for that reason.
- Live is loopback on one machine.
- The bundles are not kept: they were written to a scratch directory. Sim regenerates
  bit for bit from the files here; live does not.
