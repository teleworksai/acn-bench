## Result

- **Every twinned quantity is within its tolerance: 36 of 36** (12 cells, 3 quantities each), by the `within` flags of the committed twin, which `cargo xtask docs-inventory` recomputes from its figures (ADR-47). Every decision cell is twinned.

- **The L2 verdict is `inconclusive`** in all four slices. In `anthropic` and `openai` the reason is `twin_failed`, on 253 and 247 cells that are not twinned: they share a control arm that was run live, and HYP-22 as it stood failed a cell with one live arm. No divergence was measured on them. SPEC 080 v0.3 changes that rule (issue #81, ADR-50); these verdicts were made under v0.2 and are not re-judged.
